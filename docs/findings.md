## F-01 Quote Button Does Not Preserve Vietnamese

Status: Reproduced

User goal: A Vietnamese-speaking customer wants to discuss storage needs and request a self-storage quote without the conversation switching to English.

Preconditions:

- Candidate is using STOW at `https://stow.mystorage.vn/chat`.
- Candidate has initiated the conversation in Vietnamese.
- STOW has replied in Vietnamese.
- STOW has displayed service cards and an English "Get Self Storage quote" button.
- The candidate's registration/authentication state is unresolved.

Action sequence, candidate-reported summary (not a verbatim prompt export):

1. Candidate asked about storage in Vietnamese.
2. STOW replied in Vietnamese but displayed English service cards and an English "Get Self Storage quote" button.
3. Candidate clicked the button.
4. STOW responded in English.
5. Candidate explicitly requested Vietnamese.
6. STOW acknowledged and replied in Vietnamese.
7. Candidate clicked the quote button again.
8. Transcript showed the user message "Help me get a Self Storage quote".
9. STOW responded in English again.

Observed behavior:

- A Vietnamese conversation switched to English after the quote-button interaction.
- The transcript showed an English user message generated by the quote button: "Help me get a Self Storage quote".

Expected behavior:

- If STOW is already conversing in Vietnamese, quote-related buttons and follow-up responses should preserve Vietnamese unless the user asks to switch language.
- A quick action should not silently inject English into a Vietnamese conversation in a way that changes the assistant's response language.

Screenshot/transcript references:

- Candidate screenshots and transcript support reproduction.
- Screenshot filenames: unknown.
- Transcript export/reference: unknown.

Reproduction status:

- Reproduced based on candidate screenshots and transcript.
- Not independently reproduced by this assistant.

Customer impact:

- Low but real: Vietnamese-speaking customers may lose confidence, need to repeat language preferences, or misunderstand quote next steps.
- The issue affects a sales-relevant action, but the reported workaround is simple: ask again in Vietnamese.

Initial severity:

- Low.
- Reasoning: The behavior is frustrating and happens at a quote CTA, but there is no current evidence of blocked booking, wrong price, lost lead, data loss, or inability to continue.

Root-cause hypotheses, unverified:

- The quote button may use a hardcoded English display label and/or hardcoded English message payload.
- The assistant may infer response language from the latest generated user message rather than preserving conversation or user locale.
- The service-card component may not receive or apply the active conversation language.
- The backend/tool intent for quote collection may not carry locale metadata.

Smallest plausible fix:

- Preserve the active conversation language when rendering service-card CTAs and when sending quick-action payloads.
- For Vietnamese sessions, display a Vietnamese CTA and send a Vietnamese or locale-tagged intent such as "Tôi muốn nhận báo giá kho tự quản" or `{ intent: "self_storage_quote", locale: "vi" }`.
- Add a small regression check: after a Vietnamese prompt and quote CTA click, the visible CTA and assistant response remain Vietnamese.

Prototype suitability:

- Secondary interaction detail only: preserve Vietnamese in the selected quote-comparison flow if it fits naturally. F-02 is the primary prototype finding.

## F-02 Initial “Cheapest Option” Recommendation Omits a Cheaper Conditional Option

Status: Reproduced

Scope of status: Reproduced in the candidate session, based on candidate-reported transcript evidence; not independently reproduced by this assistant. The title summarizes the issue; the reported initial wording was "optimal cost".

Primary user goal: Choose the lowest-cost option that still supports self-access at 22:00.

Preconditions:

- Candidate is using STOW for self-storage selection.
- Candidate needs storage for six boxes.
- Candidate wants late self-access at 22:00.
- Candidate is comparing unit suitability and monthly price.

Action sequence, candidate-reported summary (not a verbatim prompt export):

1. Earlier in the conversation, STOW recommended a 2 CBM Non-AC unit at 375 Vo Nguyen Giap.
2. STOW called that 2 CBM Non-AC unit the "optimal cost" option.
3. STOW priced the initial recommendation at 1,188,000 VND/month.
4. STOW did not mention the cheaper Cool Locker at that time.
5. Later, the candidate asked why the initial recommendation was a 2 CBM Non-AC unit at 375 Vo Nguyen Giap for 1,188,000 VND/month when a smaller option might be cheaper.
6. STOW replied that Standard 1 CBM units, both Non-AC and AC, are sold out at 375 Vo Nguyen Giap and An Phu.
7. STOW replied that a 1 CBM Cool Locker at 375 Vo Nguyen Giap is available for 990,000 VND/month before 8% VAT.
8. STOW said the candidate's six boxes measure 60 x 40 x 40 cm each and have a total volume of approximately 0.58 CBM.
9. STOW claimed a typical 1 CBM locker is approximately 1 m x 1 m x 1 m, with a 0.8-0.9 m door, and that the six boxes fit.
10. In a later clarification, STOW described the Cool Locker as a repurposed Wine Locker and directed the candidate to select "Wine Storage".
11. In the latest response, STOW explicitly confirmed that clothing, books, files and dry documents are allowed if not prohibited, and confirmed private access and 24/7 self-access.
12. STOW said staff should inspect and remove racks before handover if necessary, and quoted both monthly prices before VAT: 990,000 VND for Cool Locker and 1,188,000 VND for standard 2 CBM Non-AC.

These steps are a candidate-reported sequence, not a verbatim prompt export. Exact original prompts and message timestamps remain unknown.

Observed behavior:

- STOW initially presented a 2 CBM Non-AC unit at 1,188,000 VND/month as the "optimal cost" option.
- Later, STOW presented a 1 CBM Cool Locker at 990,000 VND/month before 8% VAT.
- The later option is 198,000 VND/month cheaper before VAT, or 396,000 VND over two months before VAT.
- STOW described the later option as 24/7 and suitable for the candidate's six boxes. The latest response confirms personal-item eligibility in the conversation, subject to prohibited-item rules and staff preparation.
- The issue is premature absolute wording and incomplete comparison, not necessarily an incorrect recommendation.

Expected behavior:

- If a cheaper option may meet the same user goal, STOW should qualify the "optimal cost" claim or present the cheaper option with its conditions before recommending the more expensive unit.
- If a cheaper option has special conditions, STOW should explain why it is suitable or unsuitable for the user's stated items and access needs.

Screenshot/transcript references:

- Candidate-reported transcript sequence: CRE-003 through CRE-009 and CRE-019 in [Evidence Register](evidence-register.md#candidate-reported-evidence).
- Latest supplied response: [Latest Eligibility Response](evidence-register.md#latest-eligibility-response).
- Screenshot filenames/transcript IDs: unknown.

Reproduction status:

- Reproduced in the candidate session, based on the candidate's reported initial recommendation and later conditional alternative.
- Not independently reproduced in this task; no separate repeat session is claimed.
- Price and availability were chatbot claims at a specific time. Cool Locker was not independently confirmed as available to book. Exact message and price timestamps, physical fit and applicable terms remain unverified.

Customer impact:

- A customer could overpay or lose trust if STOW calls an option "optimal cost" while omitting a cheaper suitable option.
- The direct reported difference is 198,000 VND/month before VAT, or 396,000 VND over two months before VAT.

Initial severity:

- Medium, provisional.
- Reasoning: The incomplete comparison may affect a purchase decision by 198,000 VND/month or 396,000 VND over two months before VAT. This is potential cost exposure, not observed payment or loss; independent price, availability and suitability verification is still missing.

Root-cause hypotheses, unverified:

- STOW may rank standard unit recommendations before special product types.
- STOW may not compare all available inventory/products before using "optimal cost" language.
- STOW may treat Cool Locker as a special case and omit it unless the user challenges the recommendation.
- STOW may have had different live availability data at the earlier step.

Alternative explanation:

- Cool Locker may have special temperature conditions, so it may not be suitable for every item.
- The product may be intentionally excluded from standard recommendations unless the item type fits cold/cool storage.
- Availability may have changed between the initial recommendation and later clarification.

Smallest plausible fix:

- Present the standard option and the cheaper special option together, state temperature and eligibility conditions, and avoid calling one option "cheapest" until all eligible options have been compared.
- Explain why the special product is conditional and not automatically selected; STOW's conversational confirmation is not independent confirmation of booking terms or physical fit.
- Include price timestamp, availability source, and any product constraints in the recommendation.

Prototype suitability:

- Selected as the primary finding for a small local quote-comparison screen using captured fixture data. Source and timestamp limitations must remain visible; no live availability or booking claim is needed to demonstrate the wording/comparison improvement.
